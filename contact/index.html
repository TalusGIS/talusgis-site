Contact

Three mailboxes, each one reaching a person

There is no form on this site and nothing here runs a script. Pick the address that fits and write to it.

  • Sales and general questions info@talusgis.com
    Talus+ pricing, a conversation about whether Talus fits your department, partnership and press enquiries, anything that is not a support case. For a Talus+ quote, the quote request lists what we need to know.
  • Support and troubleshooting support@talusgis.com
    Problems with an install, an upgrade, or a runbook. Include your release version and, if you have one, the request id from the Talus Helper's support summary. The first reply may come from an automated assistant, so you get an answer quickly; it says it is AI-generated, and you can ask for a person to follow up.
  • Security disclosure security@talusgis.com
    Vulnerability reports. This mailbox goes to a person only. No automation and no AI ever touches it, as a standing rule. The disclosure policy is in SECURITY.md.

Source, bugs and suggestions

The code, the runbooks and the release notes live at github.com/TalusGIS/talus. Bug reports and suggestions go to support@talusgis.com. Every message is read by a person. Bugs are fixed in point releases, not parked; suggestions get an answer. Talus is maintained in-house by design, so outside pull requests are declined automatically; the report is the useful contribution.
