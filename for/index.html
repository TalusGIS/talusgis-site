Who it's for

Match the tool to the capacity you actually have.

Talus is for organizations that need a working GIS of points, lines, polygons, and tables, with field collection, web maps, and sharing, and do not have a GIS department to run one. Each page below says what Talus does for that kind of organization, what you need, and what it deliberately does not do, in that order.

Park districts

Trees, playgrounds, trails, and the crews who maintain them, on one map a district can run with no GIS person.

Fire protection districts

Hydrants with their test history, pre-plans attached to the building, address points you can search offline.

Rural counties

Parcels, addresses, roads, and aerials served from one machine. Validated at county scale.

Conservancies, land trusts, and non-profits

A tree inventory volunteers keep current, easements with their documents, a public map, open data.

Consultants and small GIS firms

One install per client with no license line; what the AGPL asks of you, plainly.

Organizations outside the US

Your server, your country, any coordinate system, no phone-home. What is not here yet, said up front.

Not on the list? If your organization serves points, lines, polygons, and tables and does not need the exotic 20%, Talus is probably for you too. The FAQ answers the evaluator's questions; write if yours is not there.
