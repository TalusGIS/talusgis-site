Who it's for

Match the tool to the capacity you actually have.

Talus is for organizations that need a working GIS of points, lines, polygons, and tables, with field collection, web maps, and sharing, and do not have a GIS department to run one.

No GIS department

One product, installed once, run from one admin area: users, sharing, backups, and maintenance in the same place.

One IT generalist and a virtual machine

A Linux virtual machine with four vCPUs and 8 GB, under Hyper-V, VMware, or Proxmox. You create the machine; one script installs the rest.

A small, fixed budget

Talus Community is free under the AGPL. Talus+ is one flat price per deployment, with no per-seat fees, and nothing renews on its own.

Not for you if

You need 3D, routing, the Utility Network, Parcel Fabric, or heavy imagery analytics. Talus deliberately does not do them.

If your organization serves points, lines, polygons, and tables and does not need the exotic 20%, Talus is probably for you. The FAQ answers the evaluator's questions; write if yours is not there.

See for yourself.

Download the release, read the installer and the compose file, and bring the stack up on one machine.
