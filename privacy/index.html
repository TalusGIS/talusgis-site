Privacy

The whole statement fits on one screen

  • This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts.
  • It loads nothing from third parties. Fonts and images are served from this domain. There are no embedded widgets.
  • It collects nothing from you. There are no forms. The only way to contact us is email, which you send from your own account.
  • Hosting. The site is static files served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the IP address of visitors for security purposes under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or access those logs.
  • Email. Mail you send to a talusgis.com address is handled by Google Workspace. Mail to support@ may be answered first by an automated assistant, so you get a quick reply: that reply says it is AI-generated, and you can ask for a person to follow up. To write it, your message passes through an automation service (Make) and an AI model provider (Anthropic). Mail to info@ and security@ is read by a person only. We keep mail as long as the conversation needs and use it for nothing else.
  • The product is separate. A Talus GIS installation runs on your own hardware. It has no telemetry, no update check and no license server. What an installation can reach on the internet, and how an administrator switches each thing off, is in the FAQ. What the Talus+ AI assists send and keep is described in the FAQ and governed by your agreement.
