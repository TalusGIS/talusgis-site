Talus+

Request a quote

A Talus+ quote is one page: what you are buying, what it costs, and how long the quote is good for. To write it we need a few facts. There is no form on this site. The button opens the list in your own email program, addressed to us, and you fill in the blanks.

Open the quote request in your email program

Safari asks permission before composing the email; choose Allow. If nothing opens, copy the list below into an email to info@talusgis.com. Either way the message comes from your account, and nothing is sent by this site.

About your organization

  • Organization
  • Contact: name, title, email, phone
  • Purchasing or accounts-payable contact, if different
  • How you expect to pay: PO, card, check, or ACH
  • Tax-exempt? If yes, the certificate comes with the PO
  • Term wanted: one year is standard

What happens next

You get a one-page quote back from a person, with the Talus+ line on it. How paying works is on the Editions page.
