Refunds and cancellation

Nothing renews on its own, and you keep what you installed

This page applies to Talus+. Talus Community is free and open source, so there is nothing to cancel or refund. If your signed agreement with Talus GIS LLC says something different from this page, the agreement governs.

Cancellation

  • Nothing renews automatically. We never keep a card on file and never charge without an invoice you have seen first. Each new term starts with a new quote and your approval. To cancel at the end of a term, do nothing: just don't renew.
  • What you keep. Everything you have installed keeps working: the open-source core and every Talus+ module version you have received. What ends is updates, security patches, support, and the AI assists.
  • Before the term ends. You can stop at any time. Email info@talusgis.com with your organization's name and the date you want service to end. A refund for the rest of the term is set out below.

Refunds

  • Before delivery. We invoice after your software and license file are delivered. Cancel before then and you owe nothing.
  • After delivery, half the annual Talus+ fee is refundable. Half the fee covers the Talus+ modules (dashboards, reports, story maps, and Talus Field). You keep those permanently, so that half is not refundable once they are delivered, except when a module doesn't work as documented (below). The other half covers updates, security patches, support, and the AI assists. That half is refunded for each full month left in your term.
  • How it's worked out. Refund = half the annual fee × full months remaining ÷ 12. For example, if you stop with nine full months left, the refund is 37.5% of the annual fee. The refund is based on time left, not on how much you used support or the AI assists.
  • If a module doesn't work as documented. Tell us in writing, with enough detail for us to reproduce the problem. We have 30 days to fix it, whether by a patch, by a workaround that restores the documented behavior, or by returning you to an earlier version you received that works as documented. If we can't, you can end your term and we refund the full annual fee, not just half, for each full month left. This refund never exceeds what you paid for the current term.
  • How refunds are paid. To the original payment method where possible, otherwise by ACH, within 30 days of your request.

Questions about a charge

  • Talk to us first. If an invoice or charge looks wrong, email info@talusgis.com with the invoice number. A person will reply within two business days.
  • Mistakes are ours to fix. If we billed the wrong amount, the wrong item, or the wrong organization, we send a corrected invoice or refund the difference.
  • Card disputes. If you paid by card, please contact us before disputing the charge with your card issuer. We can usually fix the problem faster than a dispute can, and without the paperwork.
