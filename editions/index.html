Two editions, one product

Talus Community and Talus+

Start with Talus Community and upgrade to Talus+ when you need it. It is the same install and the same data: the paid modules are added to what you already run, with nothing to migrate and nothing to reinstall. Talus+ is the one paid tier, and there is no other.

Talus Community

Free. AGPLv3. No AI.

  • The complete portal: catalog, sharing, groups, metadata, Open Data, administration
  • Hosted layers and tables, owner-defined schema, domains, rules, attachments, view layers
  • Bulk import at county scale, schema import and export, working-CRS support
  • Web map authoring, feature search, geocoding, streamed or self-hosted basemaps, raster store and serve
  • OGC API Features, WMTS, and GeoServices doors: ArcGIS Pro and QGIS read and edit
  • Field collection through QField over the standard doors, with a documented round-trip
  • Scheduled backups with optional encryption, off-host destination, restore drills, self-maintenance
  • The documented /api/v1 with service-account tokens
  • The invariant suite, the security overview, and every runbook

Talus+

Everything in Community, plus support, the paid modules, and the AI assists.

  • Support from the people who build it
  • Dashboards: indicators, charts, and filtered map widgets with cross-filtering
  • Reports: scheduled and per-feature PDF reports, delivered to email or webhook targets, with a locator map
  • Story maps with attachments from your own layers
  • Talus Field: a web-first field collection app that installs from the browser, with offline packs and GNSS metadata
  • AI assists: the Talus Helper, a documentation assistant any signed-in user can ask, with a diagnose mode for administrators that reads a health snapshot they preview before it is sent; it never runs anything
  • A commercial license for the identical core, for procurement policies that cannot accept the AGPL

Ask about Talus+

Stop paying if you want: keep everything installed, forever. The paid modules you have installed stay installed if your subscription ends. There is no kill switch, no phone-home, and no license server that can turn your GIS off.

Air-gapped is a deployment mode, not an edition

Either edition runs with no outbound network at all. Set map backgrounds to Downloaded, remove the public address search, and configure none of the optional outbound connections: the stack then makes none, and no viewer's browser is sent past your network, which you can confirm at your own network layer. An air-gapped Talus+ keeps every non-AI module and reports the AI assists as not available in this environment.

Pricing

Talus+ is $4,999 per deployment per year, for terms starting through December 31, 2027. One flat, public price: no per-seat meters, no negotiation. Ask and you will get a one-page quote and an explanation of what it covers. We do not gouge, upsell, or negotiate price.

How to pay

  • Ask for a quote. Email info@talusgis.com, or start from the quote request, which lists what we need to know. You will get back a one-page quote that says what you are buying, what it costs, and how long the quote is good for.
  • We accept PO, credit card, check, and ACH. We send the invoice once your software and license file have been delivered.
  • Tax-exempt buyers. If your organization is exempt from sales tax, you must send your exemption certificate with the PO.
  • Renew, or not. Before your term ends you will get a renewal quote, and it works the same way. If you decide not to renew, everything you have installed keeps working. You stop receiving updates, support, and the AI assists, but nothing else changes. Stopping partway through a term, and what is refunded, is on the refunds and cancellation page.

No surprise bills. The AI assists come with a generous included allowance for the year. If your deployment ever reaches it, the AI pauses and we get in touch to sort it out. There is no meter running behind any feature and no charge that you did not approve first.

Licensing, briefly

  • Using Talus obligates you to publish nothing. AGPL obligations trigger on modifying and distributing or serving the software, not on running it. An organization running Talus, even public-facing, has no disclosure obligation.
  • Why AGPL and not permissive? So the open core stays open. No vendor can absorb Talus into a closed product and sell it back to you. It protects your exit rights.
  • Procurement bans the AGPL outright? A commercial license for the identical code is available directly from Talus GIS LLC, which holds the copyright. Same software, different paper.
